Why Lonavala needs a different approach

Lonavala and Khandala sit on the Sahyadri ghats and receive some of the heaviest monsoon rainfall in Maharashtra. Many properties are second homes that stay closed for weeks at a time. Together, that creates problems that city flats rarely have.

  • Termites thrive in damp timber, and bungalows here often have wooden floors, roofs, panelling and furniture. Dampwood termites attack wood softened by leaks; subterranean termites come up from the soil around the house.
  • Rodents move into closed homes during the rains, nesting in roofs, store rooms and furniture and chewing through wiring.
  • Mosquitoes breed in the water that collects in gutters, garden features, unused pools and containers left outside.
  • Bed bugs and cockroaches are a recurring issue for resorts, homestays and villas with a steady turnover of guests.

The two visits that matter most

Before the monsoon (May): termite inspection and treatment, rodent proofing of roofs and vents, and clearing places where water will collect. After the monsoon (October): check timber for termite and damp damage, remove nests, and treat any cockroach or bed bug activity before the holiday season.

For holiday homes we can carry out a visit while you are away if a caretaker can give access, and send you the treatment certificate afterwards.

Resorts, hotels and homestays

Hospitality properties need a year-round programme: regular cockroach and rodent control in kitchens and stores, bed bug inspections of rooms, and mosquito control in gardens and around pools. Every visit is documented with a treatment certificate, so your records are ready for FSSAI inspections and guest complaints.

How a treatment works

  1. Inspection. The technician identifies the pest, finds where it is living and how it is getting in, and judges how severe the infestation is.
  2. Treatment plan and quote. You get an itemised quote and a plan: which products, where they go, and whether a follow-up visit is needed.
  3. Treatment. Trained, background-verified technicians carry out the work with WHO and CIB-approved products and tell you about any precautions.
  4. Certificate and follow-up. You receive a treatment certificate after every visit. AMC clients get scheduled follow-up visits and re-treatment at no extra cost between visits.
