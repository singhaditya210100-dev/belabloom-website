Areas We Cover

Pest control across South & Central Mumbai

Worli

Sea-facing towers, the BDD chawls under redevelopment, offices along Annie Besant Road and the Worli Koliwada fishing village all sit within a few kilometres. Demolition and excavation on redevelopment sites push rats into neighbouring buildings, so we pay close attention to ground floors, basements and garbage chutes. In towers, cockroaches travel between flats through plumbing shafts, so shaft treatment matters as much as treating each kitchen.

Dadar

Dense residential lanes around Shivaji Park and Hindu Colony, the markets near the station and a high concentration of eateries make Dadar one of the busiest pest environments in the city. Many buildings are several decades old, with shared drainage stacks and wooden doors, beams and lofts. That means German cockroaches in kitchens, rats around markets and gullies, and drywood termites in old timber.

What drives pest problems in South and Central Mumbai

The island city has some of Mumbai's oldest housing stock: cessed buildings, chawls and pre-war apartment blocks, many of them now being redeveloped next to brand-new towers. The two kinds of building fail in different ways.

  • Older buildings share drainage stacks, ducts and gullies, the narrow service passages between buildings. Cockroaches and rats move freely between flats, so treating one flat while the neighbours don't is a common reason treatments "stop working" after a few weeks. Old wooden staircases, doors and lofts also give termites a foothold.
  • New towers have plumbing and electrical shafts, false ceilings, podium parking and basements. Pests use the shafts as highways between floors, and the basement and garbage room are where rodent problems start.
  • Redevelopment sites hold water in curing ponds, lift pits and debris during the monsoon, which is exactly where the urban malaria mosquito, Anopheles stephensi, breeds. The BMC's insecticide department inspects construction sites for breeding.
  • Offices and restaurants in Worli and Lower Parel need treatment that works around opening hours and produces paperwork for audits and food-safety inspections.

How we approach a building here

For a single flat we treat the kitchen, bathrooms and entry points and tell you honestly if the source is in a shared duct. For a housing society we quote for the common areas too: drainage chambers, shafts, the garbage area, the meter room and the terrace tank surroundings. Treating these once a quarter under an annual maintenance contract (AMC) is usually cheaper than repeated call-outs from individual flats.

How a treatment works

  1. Inspection. The technician identifies the pest, finds where it is living and how it is getting in, and judges how severe the infestation is.
  2. Treatment plan and quote. You get an itemised quote and a plan: which products, where they go, and whether a follow-up visit is needed.
  3. Treatment. Trained, background-verified technicians carry out the work with WHO and CIB-approved products and tell you about any precautions.
  4. Certificate and follow-up. You receive a treatment certificate after every visit. AMC clients get scheduled follow-up visits and re-treatment at no extra cost between visits.
FAQs

Pest control in South & Central Mumbai: common questions

How much does pest control cost in Worli or Dadar?

Prices start at Rs 499 for a cockroach treatment and go up to Rs 3,999 for a comprehensive AMC. The final quote depends on the size of the flat or office and how severe the problem is. We give an itemised quote before any work starts.

I live in an old building. Is it worth treating just my flat?

Yes, a gel-bait treatment will clear the cockroaches living in your flat. But if the building shares drainage stacks or ducts, insects can return from neighbouring flats. If that is happening, we will tell you and can quote for the society's common areas.

Can you treat our office or restaurant without disrupting business?

Yes. Commercial treatments are planned around your working hours, and you receive a treatment certificate after every visit for audit and food-safety records.

Is the treatment safe for children, elderly family members and pets?

We use WHO and CIB-approved products, and gel baits are placed in cracks and hinges out of reach. The technician will tell you about any precautions for your home, such as how long to keep pets out of a treated room.
